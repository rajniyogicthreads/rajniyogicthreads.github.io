Our Services

Heal with Yoga Therapy

Heal from lifestyle disorder diseases with our comprehensive yoga techniques and pranayama programs. Gain the skills and confidence to handle stress and inspire others on their wellness path.

Unlike general yoga classes, yoga therapy is tailored to your unique needs, abilities, and health history — combining gentle asana, breath work and deep relaxation into a plan built around you.

Meditation Sessions

Join our guided meditation sessions to cultivate mindfulness and reduce stress.

In a world full of noise and distractions, meditation offers a path back to yourself. Whether you're seeking more calm, clarity, or connection, our guided meditation sessions create a gentle space to slow down and return to the present moment.

Kids Yoga — Nurturing Young Minds & Bodies

Our specially designed classes help children build strength, focus, coordination, and self-awareness in a fun, safe, and nurturing environment.

Each session combines age-appropriate poses, breathing exercises, games, and storytelling to spark curiosity and confidence.

Private Yoga Sessions

Enjoy personalized yoga sessions designed to meet your unique needs and goals. Our experienced instructors will work with you one-on-one to deepen your practice and enhance your wellness journey.

Group Yoga Sessions

Looking to reconnect with your body, calm your mind, and be part of a supportive community? Our group yoga sessions offer the perfect opportunity to stretch, strengthen, and center yourself — whether you're new to yoga or a seasoned practitioner.

Yoga Workshops — Explore, Learn, Transform

Our yoga workshops are designed for those ready to go deeper — whether you're exploring a specific style, building on your foundations, or reconnecting with your practice in a new way.

Each workshop offers a unique experience, with a focused theme and plenty of time to ask questions and practise at your own pace.

Meet Your Instructor

Hi, I'm Rajni Mundhra — a certified yoga instructor with around three years of teaching experience across Hatha, Vinyasa, Therapy, Restorative Yoga and Pilates.

I'm passionate about fostering mental and physical wellness, and committed to helping students develop strength, flexibility and mindfulness. Every sequence I teach is built around the person in front of me — their goals, their body, and a safe, supportive space to practise in.
